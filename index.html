Our Story

Born in Oregon. Built for the Pacific.

Western Air is a proposed full-service international airline headquartered in Portland, Oregon, with its main hub at Portland International Airport (PDX).

Our ambition is to make Portland a gateway between North America, Asia, Oceania, and the Pacific Islands—bringing people, cultures, and opportunities closer together.

Inspired by the spirit of exploration that defines the Pacific Northwest, we believe the journey should be as memorable as the destination.

Our Mission

Connect the communities and destinations of the Pacific through safe, reliable, comfortable, and exceptional air travel.

Our Vision

Establish Portland as a leading international gateway to the Pacific.
